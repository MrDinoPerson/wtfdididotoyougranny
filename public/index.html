GRANNY'S HOUSE

Find 3 keys (one hides on each floor: basement, ground, upstairs), then unlock the exit on the ground floor, bottom-right room. Walk onto the stairs to change floors. Granny uses them too. Harder modes add locked gates (hold E), breakers, and flashlight batteries.
WASD move · Shift sprint (loud!) · Mouse aim flashlight · E pick up / hide / leave locker · Hold E revive a friend.
